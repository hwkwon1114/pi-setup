# Current investigation

Optional starting sections; adapt to experiments, theory or diagnosis. May share
a document with the objective. A proposal is not launch approval.

- **Status and objective link:** Proposed / active / paused / complete for scope / unresolved.
- **Question and purpose connection:** Which part of the long-term purpose does this address?
- **Role:** Direct evidence / supporting diagnostic (name dependency) / separate proposed question.
- **Competing explanations:** What must the evidence distinguish?
- **Smallest useful design:** Test, argument or source inspection; controls/comparisons as applicable.
- **Information and assumptions:** Allowed inputs, data provenance, independent units and uncertainty where applicable.
- **Evidence versus proxies:** Target evidence, diagnostics and what each cannot establish.

- **Conclusion and claim boundary:** Answer to the original question, alternative explanations and limits.
- **Changes and handoff:** Rationale, rejected directions, unresolved issues and next authorized action.
