# Research purpose

Optional starting sections; reuse existing project notes and omit inapplicable
fields. This draft does not grant execution permission.

- **Direction and provenance:** Researcher's request/date; explicit versus provisional details.
- **Long-term purpose:** What do we want to understand or change, and why?
- **Target and domain:** Claim, quantity or property; conditions and assumptions.
- **Evidence for progress:** What would answer or narrow the question? What cannot establish it?
- **Targets versus proxies:** Primary evidence; diagnostic/optimization metrics if relevant; connection and limitations.
- **Non-goals and drift traps:** What would distract from this purpose?
- **Current investigation:** Existing path or section; next decision it addresses.
- **Constraints:** Applicable data, ethics, safety and host requirements; no invented fixed budget.
- **Revision history:** Explicit changes of direction, rationale and preserved prior purpose.
