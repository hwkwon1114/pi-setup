# Decisions — current rules

One line per rule that still governs future work, in plain words, with its decision ID(s). The full wording,
rationale, source and status of every decision are kept verbatim in `DECISIONS_LOG.md`. **Before relying on a rule
for a consequential choice, or when a card cites an ID, read its full row there; on any conflict the full row
wins.** Cards cite IDs instead of restating rules.

New decision: append the full row to `DECISIONS_LOG.md` (next ID D02) and add or replace its line here. When a rule
is superseded, mark its log row `superseded → D-xx` (never delete it) and remove or replace its line here.
